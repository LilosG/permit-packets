What it handles

Four jobs, one review step

Each job type has its own packet, built from the same walkthrough notes and checked the same way.

EV charger

Application, site plan, one-line diagram, load calculation and equipment sheets for a Level 2 charger.

Panel / service upgrade

Load calculation, riser diagram, site plan and the utility service request for a new service or panel.

Subpanel / feeder

Feeder sizing and schedule, voltage drop, one-line diagram, load calculation and site plan.

240 V circuits

Hot tubs, saunas, heat pumps, mini-splits

Circuit sizing, a one-line diagram with the circuit schedule, load calculation and site plan.

How it works

From the driveway to the permit office

  1. On site

    Pick the job

    Choose EV charger, panel or service upgrade, subpanel or 240 V circuit.

  2. On site

    Capture the job

    Fill in a short form on your phone: address, equipment, panel, wire run and photos.

  3. Automatic

    Build the packet

    The system runs the numbers, drafts the paperwork and flags anything missing.

  4. You

    Review and approve

    Check everything, fix what's flagged and sign off. Nothing moves without you.

  5. You

    Submit

    Send it to your city, or have your office do it, and track where it stands.
